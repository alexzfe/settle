# Shopping Guides: Fixture Home

## Wool rug

Living room, Locked

A large wool rug under the sofa.

### Quick Guide

**Looking for:** Wool · cut pile · warm terracotta · 2.0–3.4 m long

#### Measure first

- Living room · Wall 5 · length. Recorded **~370 cm** (estimate). Measured: ____ cm

#### Must

- At least 2.0 × 1.4 m
- No longer than 3.4 m, to keep the walk past the west window clear

#### Avoid

- Loop pile, viscose blends — claws catch, viscose marks

#### Prefer

- Wool, low pile: loops catch the cats' claws
- Warm terracotta, the Palette's accent, or close to it

#### In the shop

- Rub the pile hard: more than a little fluff means shedding
- Turn a corner back: stiff latex cracks on tiles

#### Ask the seller

- Backing latex or felt?

### Full Guide

_Written 2026-09-14._

#### Size

- **At least 2.0 × 1.4 m** (must). The sofa's front legs should stand on the rug, or it floats
  and the seating looks smaller; the wall behind it is 2.1 m, as printed on the plan.
- **No longer than 3.4 m** (must). About 30 cm must stay clear along the west window wall to
  walk past. That wall was only estimated at 3.7 m, so measure it before buying.

#### Material

- **Wool, low pile** (prefer). Two cats live here: claws catch in loops, and wool shrugs off
  scratching better than linen or viscose.

#### Color

- **Warm terracotta** (prefer). The accent of the Warm clay Palette, echoing the dining-end
  tiles; a rug is the one large surface in the room that can carry it.
