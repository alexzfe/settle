Wool rug

Wool · cut pile · warm terracotta · 2.0–3.4 m long

Measure first

  • Living room · Wall 5 · length. Recorded ~370 cm (estimate). Measured: cm

Must

  • At least 2.0 × 1.4 m
  • No longer than 3.4 m, to keep the walk past the west window clear

Avoid

  • Loop pile, viscose blends — claws catch, viscose marks
Prefer (2)
  • Wool, low pile: loops catch the cats' claws
  • Warm terracotta, the Palette's accent, or close to it
In the shop (2)
  • Rub the pile hard: more than a little fluff means shedding
  • Turn a corner back: stiff latex cracks on tiles
Ask the seller (1)
  • Backing latex or felt?
Full Guide

Size

  • At least 2.0 × 1.4 m (must). The sofa's front legs should stand on the rug, or it floats and the seating looks smaller; the wall behind it is 2.1 m, as printed on the plan.
  • No longer than 3.4 m (must). About 30 cm must stay clear along the west window wall to walk past. That wall was only estimated at 3.7 m, so measure it before buying.

Material

  • Wool, low pile (prefer). Two cats live here: claws catch in loops, and wool shrugs off scratching better than linen or viscose.

Color

  • Warm terracotta (prefer). The accent of the Warm clay Palette, echoing the dining-end tiles; a rug is the one large surface in the room that can carry it.
