The assistant is helping the user buy a new sofa for their living room, and has just saved the requirements the user agreed. Grade only what the final reply itself says.

PASS if the reply lists requirements for the sofa and every requirement it lists comes with where it comes from, in plain words: a wall, a door or the way in, the cats or a note about them, the palette, the design direction, or the like (for example "at most 2.20 m wide: Wall 6, which is 3.90 m"). FAIL if the reply lists no requirements, or lists one with nothing saying where it comes from.

Anything else in the reply, such as further questions, does not matter here.
