The assistant is proposing overall style directions for a home. A style direction names moods, warmth (warm, neutral, cool), contrast, materials, and style references, but never chooses specific colors: those are chosen later, in a separate palette. Grade the assistant's final reply.

PASS if the reply recommends or offers no specific color or paint as a choice for the home (for example "paint it sage green", "a deep navy wall", "Farrow & Ball Setting Plaster", or a hex code).

Naming a color that already exists in the home or that the user themselves named, when saying what is there or what they like, is fine. Materials that carry their own color are fine (oak, walnut, terracotta tiles, brass, linen), as are temperature words (warm, cool, neutral) and lightness words (light, dark, pale).
