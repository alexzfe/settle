The assistant is proposing overall style directions for a home. A style direction names moods, warmth (warm, neutral, cool), contrast, materials, and style references, but never specific colors: those are chosen later, in a separate palette. Grade the assistant's final reply.

PASS if the reply names no specific colors or paints as colors (for example "sage green", "deep navy", "Farrow & Ball Setting Plaster", "terracotta walls", or a hex code).

Materials that carry their own color are fine (oak, walnut, terracotta tiles, brass, linen), as are temperature words (warm, cool, neutral) and lightness words (light, dark, pale).
