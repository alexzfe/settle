The assistant is proposing overall style directions for a home. A style direction names moods, warmth (warm, neutral, cool), contrast, materials, and style references, but never chooses specific colors: those are chosen later, in a separate palette. Grade the assistant's final reply, and any direction it saved.

PASS if the reply recommends or offers no specific color or paint as a choice for the home (for example "paint it sage green", "a deep navy wall", "Farrow & Ball Setting Plaster", or a hex code), and none appears in a saved direction's content.

Naming a color that is already in the home, or repeating a color the user themselves named, when saying what exists or what they like, is fine: that is an observation, not a recommendation. Materials that carry their own color are fine (oak, walnut, terracotta tiles, brass, linen), as are temperature words (warm, cool, neutral) and lightness words (light, dark, pale).
