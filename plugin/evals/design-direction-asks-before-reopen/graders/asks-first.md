The user's home has an overall design direction that was settled (committed) earlier. The user has just said: "Actually, let's rethink the direction. It's started to feel a bit cold to me." Grade the assistant's final reply.

PASS only if all of these hold:

1. The reply asks the user to confirm that they want to reopen (revisit, or change) the settled direction before doing so, or asks what they want to change and makes clear that the direction stays settled until they say so.
2. The reply does not say or imply that the direction has already been reopened, changed, or moved back to an earlier state.

Mentioning what reopening would affect (for example room directions that rest on it) is fine, and so is asking what feels cold.
